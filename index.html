TagBoss – Test & Tag Manager Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to TagBoss – Test & Tag Manager, published by TagBoss Software.

Privacy enquiries: tagboss.support@gmail.com

What TagBoss stores

TagBoss is an offline-first recordkeeping and label-management app for electrical test-and-tag work. Information entered into TagBoss may include tester name, licence or competency identification, company name, customer, site and location information, equipment descriptions, make, model, serial number, notes, permanent item identifiers, test dates, retest dates, PASS/FAIL results, failure notes, correction history, printer settings, print-job history and backup metadata.

This operational information is stored in TagBoss private application storage on the user's device. TagBoss does not require a TagBoss account or cloud login.

Camera access

TagBoss uses camera access so the user can scan TagBoss QR codes. QR scanning is used for app functionality. The current release does not upload camera images or scanned QR content to TagBoss servers.

Bluetooth and printing

TagBoss uses Bluetooth permissions so the user can discover, connect to and print to supported Brother label printers. Label data sent to a printer can include information selected for the printed tag, such as item identification, test dates, tester details and the TagBoss QR code.

Printer communication is initiated by the user and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Network access, analytics and advertising

The current TagBoss release does not request Android INTERNET permission. It does not include analytics SDKs, third-party crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs or cloud synchronisation.

If a future release adds advertising or other network-connected services, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before that release is distributed.

Exports, sharing and backups

TagBoss lets users deliberately save or share reports, exports and full backups. Exported files can contain personal or commercial information from the user's records. When the user chooses to save or share a file outside TagBoss, the destination selected by the user controls that external copy under its own terms and privacy practices.

TagBoss cannot revoke or delete copies already saved or shared outside the app. Full TagBoss backup files are not encrypted by TagBoss in the current design, so users should store backups in a suitably protected location.

Data retention and deletion

Operational records remain in the local database until changed or removed through TagBoss functionality, replaced by a restore operation, or removed when the app's private storage is deleted.

TagBoss intentionally retains completed historical test evidence for record integrity. If an item is permanently deleted, the item is removed from normal use but its permanent identifier and completed historical test records are retained so earlier reports remain accurate.

Uninstalling TagBoss removes the app's private on-device database. Files previously exported, saved or shared outside TagBoss are not removed by uninstalling the app.

TagBoss does not currently provide an online user account, so there is no server-side TagBoss account or cloud record store to delete.

Data security

TagBoss stores its operational database in Android private application storage. Android cloud auto-backup is disabled for TagBoss and cleartext network traffic is disabled.

Users are responsible for protecting files after they deliberately export, save or share them outside TagBoss.

Intended audience

TagBoss is a workplace recordkeeping tool intended for people aged 16 and over who use it for electrical test-and-tag activities. It is not designed for children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if TagBoss features, third-party SDKs, data handling or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to tagboss.support@gmail.com.
